# Weekly Workflow

Applies once campaigns are live (Phase 5+). Run on a fixed day each week
(recommend Monday, reviewing the prior Mon–Sun window) so reports are
always comparable week over week.

## The weekly block (~60–90 min)

1. **Run `03-sops/sop-weekly-optimization.md` in full** — this is the
   substance of the weekly cycle: numbers pulled, search terms reviewed,
   ad/keyword performance reviewed, budget pacing checked.
2. **Check the CRO experiment backlog**
   (`02-playbooks/landing-page-cro-playbook.md`) — read out any test that
   hit its minimum duration/sample size; start the next-priority test if
   the current one just concluded.
3. **Reconcile lead data quality** — spot-check a sample of the week's
   leads in `/admin` against `03-sops/sop-lead-followup.md`'s spam-flagging
   guidance so qualified-lead counts in the report are trustworthy.
4. **Publish the weekly report** using
   `07-reporting/weekly-report-template.md`.
5. **Carry forward open items** — anything flagged this week that wasn't
   actioned (a founder approval needed, a landing page fix blocked on
   engineering time) gets explicitly listed at the top of next week's
   report until it's resolved, not silently dropped.

## Pre-flight, before Phase 5 is even live

Until there's a campaign to review, use the weekly slot for roadmap
progress instead: which phase is in flight, what's blocking it, what's
needed from the founder to move forward. Same cadence, different content —
don't let the week pass with silence just because ads aren't live yet.
