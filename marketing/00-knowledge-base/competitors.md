## Category shape

The India stock-market-education space is dominated by **free or
near-free content platforms**, not paid 1:1 mentorship. That's the single
most important competitive fact for StockSense: it is not competing
primarily on price or content depth, it's competing against the *default
choice* being "just watch free videos."

## Direct/adjacent players observed

| Player | Model | Price signal | Where StockSense differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zerodha Varsity** | Free, self-paced written modules (basics → derivatives → taxation) | Free | No 1:1 human contact at all; StockSense's entire pitch is a real conversation, not more reading |
| **Groww Academy** | Free content, tied to Groww's brokerage app | Free | Same content-not-conversation gap; also has an obvious brokerage upsell motive StockSense can credibly say it doesn't have |
| **Elearnmarkets (StockEdge)** | Paid structured courses + tools | ₹thousands–₹tens of thousands | Closer analog in seriousness, but course-based not mentorship-based |
| **Get Together Finance (GTF)** | Free course ("Trading in the Zone – Elementary") + social/demo funnel | Free / lead-gen via demo sessions | Uses the same "free demo session" funnel mechanic StockSense should study closely — see notes below |
| **IFMC Institute / ICFM India** | Institute-style paid courses, often city-based/offline-rooted | Paid, wide range | Positions as an "institute," more formal/credential-flavored than StockSense's calm-mentor tone |
| **Nifty Trading Academy** | Paid courses with a free demo class as the funnel | Paid, free demo entry point | Nearly identical funnel shape to StockSense (free session → paid relationship) — direct structural competitor worth periodic ad-copy comparison |
| **NISM (official)** | Free, certification-flavored, regulator-adjacent | Free | Carries implicit authority/legitimacy StockSense can't claim (not SEBI-registered) — don't compete on authority, compete on approachability |

*(Sourced from a live web search on 2026-08-06; verify current offers and
pricing before quoting any competitor in ad copy — do not name competitors
in public-facing copy without a compliance check.)*

## What this means for positioning

1. **Don't compete on "more content."** Every free-content competitor above
   has more volume than StockSense will ever produce. Compete on the
   qualifying conversation instead.
2. **The free-intro-session funnel mechanic is not novel** (GTF, Nifty
   Trading Academy already run it) — the differentiator has to be in what
   happens *during* the call and how the landing page sets that expectation,
   not the mechanic itself.
3. **"Not SEBI-registered" is a real disclosure NISM-adjacent competitors
   don't have to make.** Don't hide it — the honesty is on-brand — but ad
   copy and landing content should make the *educational, non-advisory*
   framing feel like a deliberate choice (calm, no-tips, no-pressure) rather
   than a limitation.
4. **Beginner-vs-serious segmentation matters more here than course
   breadth.** Competitors mostly serve one broad "learn trading" audience;
   StockSense's persona set (`icp-personas.md`) is narrower and more
   conversation-shaped — lean into that in ad group structure rather than
   trying to out-content the free platforms.
