# SOP — Ad Copy Review

**Owner:** Copywriting Expert (drafts), CMO (final sign-off per
`01-agent-architecture/raci.md`)
**When to use:** Before any ad copy — new or edited — goes live, and
before any landing page copy change ships.

## Steps

1. **Draft using `06-prompts/ad-copy-generator.md`**, grounded explicitly
   in the target persona from `00-knowledge-base/icp-personas.md` and the
   angle guidance in `02-playbooks/copywriting-playbook.md`.
2. **Self-check against `05-checklists/ad-copy-checklist.md`** — every box,
   every time, including for a one-word tweak.
3. **Cross-check claims.** Any number, stat, or outcome claim ("2,000+
   learners," "30-minute session") must be verifiable against actual data
   (`00-knowledge-base/brand.md` guardrails) — if it can't be verified
   right now, cut it or flag it to the founder rather than shipping an
   estimate.
4. **Voice check against `brand.md`.** Read it back — does this sound like
   "calm, not hyped," or has urgency/salesy language crept in? Cut anything
   that reads as pressure.
5. **Message-match check.** Does the ad's promise match what the specific
   landing destination actually shows above the fold? Mismatched promises
   hurt Quality Score and burn the click.
6. **Compliance re-read.** No "tips," "signal," "recommendation," or
   implied-guarantee language anywhere in headline, description, or any
   extension text.
7. **CMO sign-off** before the copy goes live — log the approval (who,
   date) in the campaign notes referenced from
   `07-reporting/weekly-report-template.md`.

## When editing existing live copy

Treat an edit to already-approved copy with the same rigor as new copy —
re-run steps 2–6 in full. Voice and compliance drift happens through small,
individually-reasonable-seeming edits, not through one bad draft.
