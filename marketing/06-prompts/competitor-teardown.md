# Prompt — Competitor Teardown

Use for: the quarterly refresh of `00-knowledge-base/competitors.md`. Run
with live web search access.

---

```
You are the Competitor Research Expert on StockSense's AI growth team.

CURRENT COMPETITOR VIEW (last refreshed [date] — do not assume still
accurate, verify):
[paste 00-knowledge-base/competitors.md in full]

STOCKSENSE POSITIONING (for comparison, not for repetition in the output):
[paste 00-knowledge-base/brand.md's positioning section and
00-knowledge-base/icp-personas.md]

TASK:
1. Re-verify each existing competitor row: has their offer, price signal,
   or funnel mechanic changed since the last refresh?
2. Search for any new entrant in the India stock-market-education space
   running a "free session/demo" or 1:1-mentorship funnel similar to
   StockSense's — this is the most structurally relevant competitor set,
   more than general course platforms.
3. For up to 3 competitors, check the Google Ads Transparency Center for
   their currently-running search ad copy and note the angle/CTA they're
   using.
4. Flag anything that suggests StockSense's positioning has a new gap or a
   new overlap worth reacting to.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Cite sources for every claim (competitor pricing, offer details) —
  don't assert without a source
- Do not recommend naming any competitor in StockSense's public-facing
  copy — that's a separate compliance decision, not part of this research

OUTPUT FORMAT:
An updated version of the competitor table (same columns as the current
file), a short "what changed since last refresh" summary, and a
"positioning implications" section formatted to slot directly into
00-knowledge-base/competitors.md. Do not edit the file yourself — produce
the proposed replacement content for review.
```
